- **Lists and search no longer show the wrong thing while they load** (issues #109 and #112).
  Opening a genre, label, festival, podcast, year or lineup used to show the previous list under
  the new name for a moment; it now shows placeholder rows until its own tracks arrive, and going
  back to one seen in the last few minutes is instant. Search says it is searching instead of "No
  results" while it waits, keeps the previous results dimmed as you type, and offers a retry when
  the search fails. History, Favourites and Memories show placeholders while loading instead of a
  spinner.
